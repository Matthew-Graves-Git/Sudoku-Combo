1. Introduction

Sudoku Combo (“Sudoku Combo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a free Discord gaming application independently operated by an individual developer.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Combo collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you use its slash commands, Sudoku games, leaderboards, multiplayer features, and challenges (collectively, the “Service”).

We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide Sudoku Combo's features.

2. Information We Collect
A. Discord User IDs

We store your Discord user ID to recognize your account and associate your Sudoku scores and statistics with you.

Your Discord user ID is a persistent identifier. Although it is not your real name, it can be used to distinguish your account and may constitute personal information under applicable privacy laws.

We do not need your Discord password to operate Sudoku Combo.

B. Sudoku Scores and Statistics

We store gameplay information necessary to provide the Service, which may include:

Sudoku scores and results.
Statistics used to calculate performance or rankings.
Information needed to support leaderboards and multiplayer challenges.

The specific records stored depend on the features implemented in the application.

We use this information to save gameplay results, maintain leaderboards, facilitate challenges, and provide a consistent gaming experience.

C. Slash Commands and Interactions

When you use Sudoku Combo's slash commands or interactive features, Discord provides information needed for the application to process your request.

We process relevant interaction data to execute commands, identify the user, and return the requested game response.

We do not intentionally store general Discord message content, private conversations, passwords, email addresses, or precise location data as part of Sudoku Combo's stated gameplay data collection.

This statement assumes the application's code and its infrastructure do not independently collect or retain such information. Temporary processing and technical logs are addressed below.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described in this policy to:

Identify players through Discord user IDs.
Save and retrieve Sudoku scores and statistics.
Calculate and display leaderboard rankings.
Support multiplayer games and challenges.
Respond to slash commands and interactive requests.
Detect suspicious results, cheating, and misuse.
Maintain the security and reliability of the Service.
Troubleshoot technical problems and fulfill legitimate support requests.
Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and Sudoku Combo does not currently offer paid subscriptions, premium features, or in-app purchases.

We do not use the gameplay data described in this policy for advertising or behavioral advertising.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may process or disclose limited information in the following circumstances.

A. Discord

Sudoku Combo operates through Discord. Discord processes information according to its own policies and terms.

When a leaderboard or challenge result is displayed in a Discord server or channel, information included in that response may be visible to users who have access to that location.

B. Hosting and Database Providers

If we use third-party hosting, database, or infrastructure providers, those providers may process information necessary to operate the Service.

We will limit such processing to what is reasonably necessary for the provider to perform its services and will use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

The identity of any providers and the precise data they process depend on the application's actual deployment.

C. Legal and Security Reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect the rights or safety of users, investigate security incidents, or enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not otherwise share gameplay data with unrelated third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

5. Leaderboards and Multiplayer Features

Sudoku Combo includes leaderboards and multiplayer or challenge features.

Your scores and statistics may be used to calculate rankings or display game results to other players. Depending on the feature, Discord user identifiers may be used internally to associate results with the correct player, while a username or display name may be shown in the resulting message.

We do not intend to make a public directory of Discord user IDs.

Because leaderboard and challenge messages may be visible to other members of a Discord server or channel, you should not assume that information displayed through those features is private.

6. Data Retention

We retain Discord user IDs and gameplay statistics only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Service, maintain requested game records, support leaderboards and challenges, protect against abuse, and satisfy applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer necessary, we will delete it or take appropriate steps to make it no longer identifiable, subject to lawful retention requirements.

If you request deletion of your stored information, we will take reasonable steps to process the request promptly, including deleting associated gameplay records where applicable.

Information may remain temporarily in backups or limited security records until those records expire or deletion can reasonably be completed, subject to applicable law and Discord's Developer Terms.

We will not retain Discord API data longer than permitted by applicable law or Discord's applicable developer requirements.
